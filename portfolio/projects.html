MY PROJECTS

A collection of my academic projects and practical work.

Travel Management System

The Travel Management System helps users plan and organize their trips easily. It allows users to enter trip details such as destination, travel dates and total budget. Users can record their travel expenses, manage spending and track their remaining budget. The system helps users organize their travel information and manage expenses efficiently.

Hospital Management System

The Hospital Management System is designed to make hospital administration easier. It helps maintain patient details, organize hospital records and manage important information in one place. The system reduces manual paperwork and makes it easier to access and update records. Its main aim is to improve the efficiency of hospital management.

Secure Vault System for Notes

The Secure Vault System for Notes allows users to save and manage their personal notes in one place. It is designed to help users keep private information organized and protected. Users can store important notes and access them whenever required. The main objective is to provide a simple and convenient way to manage personal notes while maintaining privacy.

Online Shopping Cart

The Online Shopping Cart is designed to make online shopping easier for users. It allows users to view available products, select items and add them to their shopping cart. Users can update product quantities, remove unwanted items and calculate the total cost of their selected products. The system helps organize selected items before checkout and provides a simple shopping experience.
